05 / The Artist
“This is more than music.
This is HÉGÉMONIE.”
Derly Huncho is a Haitian AfroTrap artist bringing together Caribbean energy, modern trap production and a distinctly Haitian voice.

His music moves between confidence, romance, ambition, nightlife and raw emotion — creating a sound designed to represent a new generation while staying connected to Haitian culture.

HÉGÉMONIE represents the next chapter: a focused body of work built around presence, identity and control.
